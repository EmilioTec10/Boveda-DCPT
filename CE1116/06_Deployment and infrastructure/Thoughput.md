## 📚 Idea/Concept 

Throughput is a quality attribute that measures a system's effective processing rate, expressed in transactions, operations, or requests per second (TPS/OPS). It represents the maximum sustained capacity under a defined load and depends on optimal resource utilization, concurrency, and horizontal scalability. Its optimization requires minimizing internal complexity and identifying bottlenecks, balancing trade-offs with latency and consistency. In architecture, it is modeled within the Process View and validated through load and failover testing. Its monitoring and maintenance in production are the responsibility of Software Engineering Operations (SRE/DevOps) to ensure sustainable performance and resilience.
