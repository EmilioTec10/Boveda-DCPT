## 📚 Idea/Concept 

Application availability is a quality attribute that measures a system's ability to remain operational and accessible when users require it. It is quantified using metrics such as uptime percentage, MTBF, MTTR, and request success rate. It acts as a key architectural driver, closely tied to process and physical views, and involves a trade-off with data consistency (ACID vs. BASE models). Its management follows a risk-driven approach, where the economic impact of downtime guides the level of software integrity and the rigor of V&V. Availability is validated through operational, load, and failover testing, and is maintained through continuous monitoring with SRE/DevOps practices to meet established SLAs.
