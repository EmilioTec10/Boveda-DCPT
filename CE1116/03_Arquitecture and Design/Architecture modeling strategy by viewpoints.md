## 📚 Idea/Concept 

The viewpoint architecture modeling strategy consists of describing a system using a coherent set of views, each built according to a viewpoint that defines the conventions, elements, and notation used. This approach addresses the different concerns of stakeholders by separating them, representing specific system structures (logic, processes, development, or deployment) at different levels of abstraction. The strategy also includes rules for correspondence and traceability between views to ensure overall consistency, as well as documenting the rationale for architectural decisions. Models such as Kruchten's 4+1 or Simon Brown's C4 exemplify this practice.
